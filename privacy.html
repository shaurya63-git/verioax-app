Verioax privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Verioax is published by Shaurya Shah.

What Verioax does

Verioax checks messages, links, documents and Android apps that you submit for signs of scams, and removes personal data from them.

What we store

  • Your account: username, email address and a securely hashed password (never the password itself).
  • Your scan history: the redacted version of what you scanned and the result. Personal data such as phone numbers, account numbers and names is removed before anything is stored. The original text is never stored.
  • Android apps: only the app's fingerprint (SHA-256) and the analysis report. The app file itself is never stored, installed, run or shared.
  • Feedback you choose to send to the moderators.
  • Activity records for moderators: when you signed in, ran a scan (type and result only) or sent feedback. These never contain what you scanned.

What is sent to other services

When online checks are switched on, a link you scan may be looked up with threat-intelligence services (for example Google Safe Browsing or VirusTotal) and visited by our server in a protected way. Personal data is removed from the link first where possible. Messages, documents and app files are not sent to these services.

What we never do

We don't sell or share your data for advertising, and we don't use it to train AI models.

Your choices

You can delete scans from your history at any time. To delete your account, contact us at the address below.

Children

Verioax is not intended for children under 13.
